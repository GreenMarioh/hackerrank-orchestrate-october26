## §1.2 Hypothesis Results

### H1 — Linking window (events within ±N min of reported_at)

**Result: PARTIAL**

| Window | Resources hit / 111 total | % |
|--------|--------------------------|---|
| ±30 min | 27 | 24.3% |
| ±60 min | 58 | 52.3% |
| ±120 min | 85 | 76.6% |
| ±240 min | 85 | 76.6% |

Counter-examples: 26 of 111 expected resources (23.4%) have NO activity event in the dataset at all — they are linked only through evidence file contents (SQL, JSON, log). The time window is a **candidate-generation heuristic only, not the primary link strategy**. Evidence content linking must be the primary method.

**Implication for Phase 5:** Strategy 1 (direct identifier match in evidence files) is the primary link strategy; Strategy 4 (time-window) is secondary and must require a corroborating second signal.

### H2 — Anonymized record linking

**Result: PARTIAL**

Of 17 ANONYMIZE steps in samples:
- 4 have finding evidence from **evidence files** (evidence_03, evidence_05, evidence_94 appear as findings)
- 4 have **empty** finding evidence (cases case_03, case_04, case_21, case_25) — these are likely linked via activity or time-window, not direct identifiers

**Implication:** Anonymized records must be linkable via: (a) a direct identifier in an older snapshot before anonymization, (b) resource-ID bridging (same `resource_id` appears with identifiers in another evidence file), or (c) a `LINK_RESOURCE_IDENTITY` or catalog event. Phase 5 must implement resource-ID bridging (Strategy 3).
